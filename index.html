08 — Questions

What people ask us on the first call.

We already have a sales team. Why do we need this?

That's exactly why it helps. Your team concentrates on pitching and converting instead of researching and collecting contact details. We provide the database; your people convert it.

We already get leads from IndiaMART.

Marketplaces make you wait for enquiries. With Lead Base Pro you get a structured list of businesses to approach proactively — another acquisition channel for your sales team.

Are these genuine leads?

Our entry package gives you up to 5,000 scraped records with a minimum commitment of 3,000 genuine, authentic business leads — with available details like phone number, location, website and category.

Why should we pay for phone numbers?

Count the hours your employees spend finding businesses, numbers and locations and compiling them into sheets. We do that research for a one-time cost, so they can focus on actual sales.

Can you target a particular location?

Yes. We'll discuss your geography and industry — Bengaluru, Karnataka or other locations — depending on your requirements and data availability.

Is there a monthly subscription?

No. Every database is a one-time purchase priced by the number of leads you need.
